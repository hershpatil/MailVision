Probabilistic ML Engine
  • Text Normalization: Lowercasing, HTML tag stripping, URL normalization, entity extraction, and WordNet lemmatization.
  • TF-IDF Vectorization: Sublinear TF scaling with 1-2 n-gram combinations across a 5,000 feature sparse vocabulary.
  • True Probability Output: Returns uncompromised mathematical posterior probabilities for Spam and Ham.
Heuristic Rules & Threat Indicators
  • Urgency & Prize Scams: High-pressure phrasing ("act immediately", "claim prize", "exclusive reward").
  • Credential Harvesting: Suspicious links asking for passwords, 2FA codes, or banking verification.
  • Obfuscated Links: Detects URL shorteners (bit.ly, tinyurl), raw IPv4 URLs, and malicious character shouting.
