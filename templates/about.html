About MailVision

MailVision is a machine-learning-powered spam detection system that analyzes message content and identifies potentially unwanted or suspicious messages.

Machine Learning & Evaluation
  • Text Preprocessing: Lowercasing, HTML stripping, URL & entity tokenization, stopword filtering, and WordNet lemmatization.
  • TF-IDF Feature Extraction: Unigram and Bigram term frequency-inverse document frequency matrix construction (sublinear TF scaling).
  • Multinomial Naive Bayes: Primary deployed model for probabilistic spam classification.
  • Offline Model Evaluation: Compares Naive Bayes, Logistic Regression, and Linear SVM performance metrics (Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1 Score, Confusion Matrix) saved in model/metrics.json.
Rule Engine Heuristics
  • Urgency & Prize Detection: Scans for high-pressure wording ("act now", "claim prize", "immediate action").
  • Financial Risk Flags: Flags requests for credentials, credit cards, bank accounts, or SSNs.
  • URL & Formatting Analysis: Identifies raw IP links, domain shorteners, phone numbers, and SHOUTING caps.
